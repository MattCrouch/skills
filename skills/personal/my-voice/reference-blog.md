# Blog structure

- **Hook opens, not a thesis** — a question, confession, or scene ("Remember Ceefax?", "I'll admit it. CLIs scare me."). Never "In this post I will...".
- **Short opening paragraph** (1–3 sentences), then build out.
- **`##` headers as narrative beats** ("The problem", "The solution"), not generic labels. Listicles use `## 1. Do the thing`.
- **Reflective close** — often `## Summary`/`## Conclusion`, or a line circling back to the opening image or making a self-aware joke about your own track record. Land open or forward-looking, not a hard sell.
- **Rhetorical questions pivot sections** ("So what's the point?"), answered within a sentence or two.
- **Reference-style links** at the bottom (`[label]: url`), never inline `[text](url)`.
- **Sparse, pointed emoji** — one or two per post max, each doing a specific job (🤞, 👌, 🏃‍♂️). Never decorative, never in headers.
- **Titles sentence case by default**; listicles sometimes break to title case.
- **Bold-led bullets for parallel benefit lists**: `- **Label**. One or two plain sentences.`
- Technical posts: frame the pain in plain language before code; minimal code blocks explained in prose after; "we" for work, "I" for personal; end on an understated takeaway, not hype.
- Personal posts: ground abstract topics in a specific anecdote first; tool/product comparisons are a fair one-by-one trial with honest gripes about the winner too; open endings are fine ("Watch this space").
