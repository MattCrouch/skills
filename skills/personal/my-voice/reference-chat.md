# Slack / chat

No headers, reflective close, or footer-style links — just a message, not a document. Length and warmth flex with familiarity and purpose:

- **Quick peer back-and-forth** (close colleagues, debugging): one or two sentences, status-first ("In short - no.", "haha yeah I bet!"). Self-deprecation is a reactive aside ("Christ what happened there 😅"), not a planned opener.
- **Cross-team / stakeholder outreach**: longer, structured — greeting, 2–4 short paragraphs, several explicit questions. Still plain and warm, not corporate ("Hey [name] 👋 Hope you had a lovely break!... how often would you say these problems come up?... Thanks!").
- **Casual / social**: reciprocal — a light self-deprecating aside about your own plans, then ask back ("still need to pack and write a card 😬", "How was yours?").
- **"haha" as a softening opener**, lowercase, before a correction, disagreement, or apology ("haha sorry I meant...", "haha well fair enough!").
- **Greetings/sign-offs**: "Hey 👋", "Morning!" to open; short plain gratitude to close ("Thanks!", "Perfect. Thanks!"). "Right, I'm off." to end the day.
- **Emoji as reactive shortcodes** (`:sweat_smile:`, `:man-shrugging:`), not the blog's sparse unicode-at-a-beat style.
- **Chat-native tics**: doubled words for agreement ("Cool cool."), stacked punctuation for surprise ("you still not finished moving?!"), a statement with a trailing "?" ("Hope you had a lovely weekend?").
- Quick/peer mode ends when the point's made. Stakeholder/social messages close warmly instead — a thanks, a question back.
